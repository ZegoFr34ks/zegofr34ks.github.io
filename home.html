About YZKSTUDIOS

YZKSTUDIOS is the creative brand led by ZegoFr34ks.

Our mission is to craft original media. Be it games, videos, music, or art of all kinds. With one clear purpose: to bring joy and inspiration to people everywhere.

We believe creativity should be open and welcoming, which is why YZKSTUDIOS is committed to creating content that is freely accessible to everyone, without hiding it behind expensive paywalls.

We proudly stand beside and support every creator who shares the same heart: using art and entertainment to spread happiness and connection.
